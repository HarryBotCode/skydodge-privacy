Sky Dodge - Privacy Policy

Developer: HJ Labs · Last updated: October 25, 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Sky Dodge (package com.hjlabs.skydodge) handles information. In short: your game progress stays on your device. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device information to serve them.

Information stored on your device

To work, the game saves the following on your device only:

  • the pilot name(s) you type in (you can use any nickname),
  • scores and the on-device leaderboard,
  • stars, purchases made with in-game stars, achievements, daily rewards and mission progress,
  • your settings (sound, music, vibration, controls).

Apart from scores sent to the worldwide leaderboard (see below), the game never sends this data anywhere. It may be included in your phone's own Android backup if you have that switched on, which is handled by Google under your Google account settings. You can erase all of it at any time in Settings → Privacy → Delete all data, or by uninstalling the game.

Information we collect

HJ Labs does not collect personal data. Sky Dodge has no accounts and no analytics of its own.

Worldwide leaderboard (Google Play Games)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, Sky Dodge sends your scores to Google's Play Games Services so they can appear on the worldwide leaderboard, together with your Play Games profile name and avatar. HJ Labs does not receive your Google account details. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. You can change your Play Games profile visibility, or delete your Play Games data, in the Google Play Games settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Sky Dodge is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob: optional video ads you choose to watch for a reward (a revive, double stars, a bigger daily reward) and an occasional full-screen ad between games. Ads are never shown during play.

To show and measure ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This is done by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices:

  • If you are in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland or a US state with privacy laws, the game asks for your consent (or offers an opt-out) the first time it starts, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices.
  • On any Android device you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Permissions

  • Vibrate - for haptic feedback during play. It can be turned off in the game's settings.
  • Internet and network state - used to load ads and to send scores to the worldwide leaderboard.
  • Advertising ID - used by Google AdMob as described above.

Google Play in-app review

The game may occasionally ask Google Play to show its standard rating dialog. That dialog is provided and operated by Google; any rating or review you submit is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. The game itself never sees it.

Children

Sky Dodge is not directed at children under 13, and HJ Labs does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If a future version handles data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, with a new date at the top.
